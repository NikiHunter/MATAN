# Numeric series

> [!definition] Definition 7.1.1
> Given a sequence $(a_n)$, $n\in\mathbb{N}$, the quantity
> $$S_n=a_1+a_2+\ldots+a_n$$
> is called the partial sum of the series, the number $S=\lim_{n\to\infty}S_n$ is the sum of the series, and one writes $S:=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$. The term $a_n$ is called the general term of the series.
