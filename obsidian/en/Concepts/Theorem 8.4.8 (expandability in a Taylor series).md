# Theorem 8.4.8 (expandability in a Taylor series)

> [!theorem] Theorem 8.4.8 (expandability in a Taylor series)
> If $f\in C^\infty(A,B)$ and $|f^{(n)}(t)|\le M$ uniformly in $n$ and $t$, the Taylor series converges to $f(x)$.
