# Mean value theorem

> [!theorem] Theorem 6.5.3
> Let $E$ be open in $\mathbb{R}^n$, $s\in\mathbb{Z}_+$, and let $f: E\to\mathbb{R}$ be differentiable on $E$, $a,b\in E$ with $a\neq b$ and $\Delta_{a,b}\subset E$. Then there exists $c\in\hat{\Delta}_{a,b}$ such that
> $$f(b)-f(a)=d_cf(b-a).$$

The notes remark that this is the case $s=0$ of theorem 6.5.2, and give the example $f(x)=(\cos x,\sin x)$ showing that the corresponding statements fail for maps.
