# Theorem 10.3.1 (additivity of the integral)

> [!theorem] Theorem 10.3.1 (additivity of the integral)
> When $\mu(\Omega_1\cap\Omega_2)=0$ one has $\int_{\Omega_1\cup\Omega_2}f(x)\,dx=\int_{\Omega_1}f(x)\,dx+\int_{\Omega_2}f(x)\,dx$.
