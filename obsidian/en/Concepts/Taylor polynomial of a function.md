# Taylor polynomial of a function

> [!definition] Taylor polynomial of a function
> The partial sum $T_{n,f}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{f^{(k)}(a)}{k!}(x-a)^k$ of the Taylor series is the Taylor polynomial.
