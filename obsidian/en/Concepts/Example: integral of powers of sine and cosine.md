# Example: integral of powers of sine and cosine

> [!example] Example: integral of powers of sine and cosine
> Example using the beta function: $\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^\alpha x\cos^\beta x\,dx=\frac{\Gamma\left(\frac{\alpha+1}{2}\right)\Gamma\left(\frac{\beta+1}{2}\right)}{2\Gamma\left(\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2}+1\right)}$.
