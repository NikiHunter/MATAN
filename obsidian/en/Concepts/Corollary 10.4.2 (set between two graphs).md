# Corollary 10.4.2 (set between two graphs)

> [!theorem] Corollary 10.4.2 (set between two graphs)
> Corollary 10.4.2: for a set lying between two graphs over a bounded set, the integral reduces to an iterated one:
> $\int_{\mathcal{E}}f(x,y)\,dx\,dy=\int_{\mathcal{D}}dx\int_{\varphi_1(x)}^{\varphi_2(x)}f(x,y)\,dy$
