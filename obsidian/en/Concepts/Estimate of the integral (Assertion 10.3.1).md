# Estimate of the integral (Assertion 10.3.1)

> [!theorem] Estimate of the integral (Assertion 10.3.1)
> If $f\in R(\Omega)$, then $|f|\in R(\Omega)$ and $\left|\int_\Omega f(x)\,dx\right|\le\int_\Omega|f(x)|\,dx$.
