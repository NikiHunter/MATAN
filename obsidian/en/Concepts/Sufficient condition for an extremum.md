# Sufficient condition for an extremum

> [!theorem] Theorem 6.7.2
> Let $E\subset\mathbb{R}^n$, let $a$ be an interior point of $E$, and let $f: E\to\mathbb{R}$ be twice differentiable at $a$ with $d_af=0$. Then:
> 1) if $d_a^2f$ is positive definite, $a$ is a strict minimum point of $f$;
> 2) if $d_a^2f$ is negative definite, $a$ is a strict maximum point of $f$;
> 3) if $d_a^2f$ takes both positive and negative values, $f$ has no extremum at $a$.

> [!warning] Sketch
> In the notes the theorem is marked “sketch!” — there is no complete proof. It is also noted that the theorem does not cover the case where $d_a^2f$ keeps its sign but is not definite.
