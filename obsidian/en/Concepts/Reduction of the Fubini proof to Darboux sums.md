# Reduction of the Fubini proof to Darboux sums

> [!method] Reduction of the Fubini proof to Darboux sums
> Proof method: the lower and upper Darboux sums for a product partition bound the integral of the inner integral, after which one passes to the limit as the mesh tends to zero:
> $s(f,P)\leq\int_{I_x}F(x)\,dx\leq S(f,P)$
