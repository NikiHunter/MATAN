# Theorem 9.1.3 (integrating an integral with respect to a parameter)

> [!theorem] Theorem 9.1.3 (integrating an integral with respect to a parameter)
> For $f\in C(P)$ the equality $\int_c^d\left(\int_a^b f(x,y)\,dx\right)dy=\int_a^b\left(\int_c^d f(x,y)\,dy\right)dx$ holds.
