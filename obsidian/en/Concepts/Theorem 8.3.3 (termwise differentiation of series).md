# Theorem 8.3.3 (termwise differentiation of series)

> [!theorem] Theorem 8.3.3 (termwise differentiation of series)
> If $\sum u_n(x)$ converges and $\sum u_n'(x)$ converges uniformly on $[a,b]$, then $S\in C^1([a,b])$.
