# Integral depending on a parameter (Def. 9.1.1)

> [!definition] Integral depending on a parameter (Def. 9.1.1)
> Def. 9.1.1: an integral depending on a parameter is a function of the form $I(y)=\int f(x,y)\,dx$, where $y$ runs over some set.
