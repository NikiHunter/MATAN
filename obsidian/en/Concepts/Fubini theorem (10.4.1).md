# Fubini theorem (10.4.1)

> [!theorem] Fubini theorem (10.4.1)
> Theorem 10.4.1: the multiple integral over a rectangular interval equals the iterated integral, provided the function is integrable on the whole interval and, for each fixed value of the first variable, in the second one:
> $\int_I f(x,y)\,dx\,dy=\int_{I_x}\left(\int_{I_y}f(x,y)\,dy\right)dx$
