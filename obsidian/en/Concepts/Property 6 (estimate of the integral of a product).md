# Property 6 (estimate of the integral of a product)

> [!property] Property 6 (estimate of the integral of a product)
> Property 6: for a non-negative weight function the integral of the product is bounded below and above via the bounds of the first factor:
> $m\int_\Omega g(x)\,dx\leq\int_\Omega fg(x)\,dx\leq M\int_\Omega g(x)\,dx$
