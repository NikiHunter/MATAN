# Theorem 8.4.4 (termwise integration of power series)

> [!theorem] Theorem 8.4.4 (termwise integration of power series)
> For $[A,B]\subset(a-R,a+R)$ one has $\int_A^B\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}C_k(x-a)^k\,dx=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}C_k\frac{(B-a)^{k+1}-(A-a)^{k+1}}{k+1}$ (8.4.2).
