# Theorem 9.1.1 (continuity in the parameter)

> [!theorem] Theorem 9.1.1 (continuity in the parameter)
> For $f\in C(P)$ the function $I(y)=\int_a^b f(x,y)\,dx$ is integrable for all $y\in[c,d]$.
