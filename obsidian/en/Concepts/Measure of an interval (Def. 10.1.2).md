# Measure of an interval (Def. 10.1.2)

> [!definition] Measure of an interval (Def. 10.1.2)
> Def. 10.1.2: the number $\mu(I):=\prod_{i=1}^n(b_i-a_i)$ is the measure of the interval; it is homogeneous, additive and does not exceed the sum of the measures of a finite cover.
