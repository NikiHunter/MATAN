# Theorem 8.2.2 (passing to the limit under the integral sign)

> [!theorem] Theorem 8.2.2 (passing to the limit under the integral sign)
> Under uniform convergence $f_n\rightrightarrows f$ on $[a,b]$ one has $\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_a^b f_n(x)\,dx=\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$.
