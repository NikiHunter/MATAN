# Theorem 9.2.1 (continuity of an improper integral)

> [!theorem] Theorem 9.2.1 (continuity of an improper integral)
> If the integral $I(y)=\int_a^b f(x,y)\,dx$ converges uniformly near $y_0$, then $I(y)$ is continuous at $y_0$.
