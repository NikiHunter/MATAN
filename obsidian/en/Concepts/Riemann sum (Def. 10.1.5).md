# Riemann sum (Def. 10.1.5)

> [!definition] Riemann sum (Def. 10.1.5)
> Def. 10.1.5: the quantity $\sigma(f,P,\xi)=\sum_{i=1}^k f(\xi_i)\mu(I_i)$ is called the Riemann sum of $f$.
