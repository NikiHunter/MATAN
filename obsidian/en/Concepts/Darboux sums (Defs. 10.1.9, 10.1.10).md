# Darboux sums (Defs. 10.1.9, 10.1.10)

> [!definition] Darboux sums (Defs. 10.1.9, 10.1.10)
> With $m_i=\inf_{I_i}f$, $M_i=\sup_{I_i}f$, the quantities $s(f,P)=\sum m_i\mu(I_i)$ and $S(f,P)=\sum M_i\mu(I_i)$ are the lower and upper Darboux sums. Both definitions are marked «// допис.» in the notes.
