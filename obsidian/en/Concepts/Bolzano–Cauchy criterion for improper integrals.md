# Bolzano–Cauchy criterion for improper integrals

> [!theorem] Bolzano–Cauchy criterion for improper integrals
> Criterion: $\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists b_0\ \forall t_1,t_2\in(b_0,b)$ one has $\left|\int_{t_1}^{t_2}f(x,y)\,dx\right|<\varepsilon$.
