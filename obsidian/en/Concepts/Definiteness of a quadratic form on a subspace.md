# Definiteness of a quadratic form on a subspace

Let $N$ be a linear subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$.

> [!definition] Definition 6.7.3
> A quadratic form on $\mathbb{R}^n$ is called positive definite on $N$ if $f(x)>0$ for all $x\in N\setminus\{0\}$, and negative definite on $N$ if $f(x)<0$ for all $x\in N\setminus\{0\}$.

> [!property] Remark
> A form positive definite on $\mathbb{R}^n$ is positive definite on every linear subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$; the converse is false.
