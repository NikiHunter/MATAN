# Theorem 8.3.2 (termwise integration of series)

> [!theorem] Theorem 8.3.2 (termwise integration of series)
> If $u_n\in C([a,b])$ and the series converges uniformly to $S$, then $\int_a^b S(x)\,dx=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\int_a^b u_n(x)\,dx$.
