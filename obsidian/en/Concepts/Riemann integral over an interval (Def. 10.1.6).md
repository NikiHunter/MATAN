# Riemann integral over an interval (Def. 10.1.6)

> [!definition] Riemann integral over an interval (Def. 10.1.6)
> Def. 10.1.6: $\int_I f(x)\,dx:=\lim_{\lambda(P)\to0}\sigma(f,P,\xi)$ is the Riemann integral, provided the stated limit exists.
