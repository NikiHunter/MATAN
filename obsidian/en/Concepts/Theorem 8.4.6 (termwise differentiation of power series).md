# Theorem 8.4.6 (termwise differentiation of power series)

> [!theorem] Theorem 8.4.6 (termwise differentiation of power series)
> Inside the interval of convergence the series may be differentiated termwise any number of times: $f^{(m)}(x)=\sum_{k=m}^{\infty}k(k-1)\ldots(k-m+1)C_k(x-a)^{k-m}$.
