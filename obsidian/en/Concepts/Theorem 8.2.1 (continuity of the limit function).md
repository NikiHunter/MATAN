# Theorem 8.2.1 (continuity of the limit function)

> [!theorem] Theorem 8.2.1 (continuity of the limit function)
> If the $f_n$ are continuous and $f_n\rightrightarrows f$ on $X$, then $f$ is continuous on $X$.
