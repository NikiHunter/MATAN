# Corollary 10.4.1 (reduction to iterated integrals)

> [!theorem] Corollary 10.4.1 (reduction to iterated integrals)
> Corollary 10.4.1: for a product of intervals the multiple integral reduces to a chain of single integrals:
> $\int_I f(x)\,dx=\int_{a_n}^{b_n}dx_n\int_{a_{n-1}}^{b_{n-1}}dx_{n-1}\ldots\int_{a_1}^{b_1}f(x_1,\ldots,x_n)\,dx_1$
