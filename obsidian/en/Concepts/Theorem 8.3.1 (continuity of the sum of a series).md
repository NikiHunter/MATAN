# Theorem 8.3.1 (continuity of the sum of a series)

> [!theorem] Theorem 8.3.1 (continuity of the sum of a series)
> The sum of a uniformly convergent series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_n(x)$ of continuous functions is continuous.
