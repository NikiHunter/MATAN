# Properties of the integral (1)–(6)

> [!property] Properties of the integral (1)–(6)
> Properties of the integral: non-negativity, monotonicity, bounds via the infimum and supremum, mean value and an estimate for the integral of a product. Properties 1–3 are worked out on the board:
> $\int_\Omega f(x)\,dx\geq0$
