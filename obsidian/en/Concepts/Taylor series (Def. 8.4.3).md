# Taylor series (Def. 8.4.3)

> [!definition] Taylor series (Def. 8.4.3)
> Def. 8.4.3: the series $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{f^{(k)}(a)}{k!}(x-a)^k$ is called the Taylor series of $f$ centred at $a$; for $a=0$ it is the Maclaurin series.
