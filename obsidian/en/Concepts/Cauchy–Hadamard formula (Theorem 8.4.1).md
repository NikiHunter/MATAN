# Cauchy–Hadamard formula (Theorem 8.4.1)

> [!theorem] Cauchy–Hadamard formula (Theorem 8.4.1)
> Every power series has a radius of convergence given by $R=\dfrac{1}{\lim_{n\to\infty}\sqrt[n]{|C_n|}}$.
