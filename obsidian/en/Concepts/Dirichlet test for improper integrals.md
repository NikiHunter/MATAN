# Dirichlet test for improper integrals

> [!theorem] Dirichlet test for improper integrals
> If $f(x,y)\to0$ uniformly and $|G(t,y)|=\left|\int_a^t g(x,y)\,dx\right|\le C$, then $\int_a^bf g\,dx$ converges uniformly.
