# Notation: double and triple integrals

> [!definition] Notation: double and triple integrals
> Notation: double and triple integrals; in the two-variable case both orders of reduction to iterated integrals give the same result:
> $\iint_I f(x,y)\,dx\,dy=\int_a^b\left(\int_c^d f(x,y)\,dy\right)dx=\int_c^d\left(\int_a^b f(x,y)\,dx\right)dy$
