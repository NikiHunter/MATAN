# Power series (Def. 8.4.1)

> [!definition] Power series (Def. 8.4.1)
> Def. 8.4.1: a series of the form $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}C_k(x-a)^k$ with $C_k,x,a\in\mathbb{R}$ is called a power series; $C_k$ are its coefficients and $a$ its centre.
