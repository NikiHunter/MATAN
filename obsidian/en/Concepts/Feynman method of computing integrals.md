# Feynman method of computing integrals

> [!method] Feynman method of computing integrals
> Method: introduce a parameter $I(\alpha)=\int_0^\infty\frac{e^{-x}-e^{-\alpha x}}{x}\,dx$, differentiate with respect to it and integrate back; the notes obtain $I(2)=\ln2$.
