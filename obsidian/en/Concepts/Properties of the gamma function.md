# Properties of the gamma function

> [!property] Properties of the gamma function
> Properties: $\Gamma(1)=1$, $\Gamma(1/2)=\sqrt\pi$, $\Gamma(y+1)=y\Gamma(y)$, $\Gamma(n+1)=n!$; also Stirling’s formula and $\Gamma(y)\Gamma(1-y)=\pi/\sin(\pi y)$.
