# Uniform convergence of an improper integral (Def. 9.2.1)

> [!definition] Uniform convergence of an improper integral (Def. 9.2.1)
> Def. 9.2.1: $\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists b_0\in[a,b)\ \forall b'>b_0$ one has $|I_{b\'}(y)-I(y)|<\varepsilon$.
