# First comparison test for positive series

> [!theorem] First comparison test for positive series
> For series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ and $\sum b_n$ with $0\le a_n\le b_n$, convergence of the second implies convergence of the first, and divergence of the first implies divergence of the second.
