# Property 4 (mean value)

> [!theorem] Property 4 (mean value)
> Property 4: between the infimum and the supremum of the function there is a number for which the integral equals its product with the measure of the set:
> $\int_\Omega f(x)\,dx=\theta\mu(\Omega),\quad\theta\in[m,M]$
