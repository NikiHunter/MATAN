# Interval of convergence

> [!property] Interval of convergence
> Property: $(a-R,a+R)$ is the interval of convergence; for $R=+\infty$ it is $\mathbb{R}$, for $R=0$ it is empty, and for $0<R<+\infty$ the series converges absolutely inside it.
