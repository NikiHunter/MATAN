# Radius of convergence (Def. 8.4.2)

> [!definition] Radius of convergence (Def. 8.4.2)
> Def.: the quantity $R\in[0,+\infty]$ is the radius of convergence if the series converges for $|x-a|<R$ and diverges for $|x-a|>R$.
