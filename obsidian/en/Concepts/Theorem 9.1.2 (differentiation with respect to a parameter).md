# Theorem 9.1.2 (differentiation with respect to a parameter)

> [!theorem] Theorem 9.1.2 (differentiation with respect to a parameter)
> For $f,\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\in C(P)$ one has $I\'(y)=\int_a^b\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}(x,y)\,dx$.
