# Abel test for improper integrals

> [!theorem] Abel test for improper integrals
> If $f$ is monotone in $x$ and $|f(x,y)|\le M$, while $\int_a^bg(x,y)\,dx$ converges uniformly, then $\int_a^bf g\,dx$ converges uniformly.
