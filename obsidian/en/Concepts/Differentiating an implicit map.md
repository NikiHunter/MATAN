# Differentiating an implicit map

In the notation of theorem 6.6.1 the map $f$ is differentiable on $V(x^0,y^0)$; set $g(x)=f(x,\varphi(x))$, $x\in U$.

> [!property] Derivative formula
> $$g'(x)=f'_x(x,\varphi(x))+f'_y(x,\varphi(x))\varphi'(x).$$

The same formula appears in the notes in the proof of theorem 6.7.3, where the implicit map is denoted by $\psi$.
