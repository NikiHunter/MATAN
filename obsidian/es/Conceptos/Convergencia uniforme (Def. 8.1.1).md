# Convergencia uniforme (Def. 8.1.1)

> [!definition] Convergencia uniforme (Def. 8.1.1)
> Def. 8.1.1: $f_n\rightrightarrows f$ en $X$ si $\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists N\ \forall n>N\ \forall x\in X:\ |f_n(x)-f(x)|<\varepsilon$.
