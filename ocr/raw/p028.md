<u>Пример:</u>

$\sum\limits_{k=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{(-1)^{k-1}}{k}=1-1/2+1/3-1/4+\ldots$

$S_{2n}=1-1/2+\ldots+\dfrac{1}{2n-1}-\dfrac{1}{2n}=1+1/2+1/3+\ldots+\dfrac{1}{2n-1}+\dfrac{1}{2n}-2\left(1/2+1/4+\ldots+\dfrac{1}{2n}\right)$
$-1/2=1/2-2\cdot1/2$
$-1/4=1/4-2\cdot1/4$ ⟦S117⟧
