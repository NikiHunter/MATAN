# Mathematical Analysis, lectures 2025/26

[Русский](../../ru/lectures-2025-26/index.md) · **English** · [Español](../../es/lectures-2025-26/index.md)

These notes are transcribed from the PDF lecture files of D. E. Apushkinskaya (RUDN University). Typed slides were extracted from the PDF text layer, handwritten pages were transcribed from their images, and the figures are embedded as illustrations. Formulas use LaTeX (MathJax) and diagrams use Mermaid; both work in Obsidian and on GitHub.

**Coverage: lectures 0–50 without gaps, in Russian, English and Spanish.** Semester 1 ends with lecture 32; semester 2 spans lectures 33–64. Lectures 31–38 cover 123 pages from five PDFs and 17 figures; 39–44 cover 94 pages from three PDFs and 17 figures; 45–50 add all 84 pages from three PDFs and 4 shared illustrations. In total, lectures 31–50 comprise 301 pages from 11 PDFs and 38 shared JPEG crops; every final crop was opened individually. Manifests: [31–38](../../assets/lectures-2025-26/figures-31-38.json), [39–44](../../assets/lectures-2025-26/figures-39-44.json), [45–50](../../assets/lectures-2025-26/figures-45-50.json); [extraction and review of this batch](../../assets/lectures-2025-26/provenance-45-50.md).

## Conventions

- Numbers of definitions, theorems and corollaries are kept as in the source slides. In lectures 5–6 the label "Definition 1.3.5" is repeated three times; see the note in the file.
- Handwritten pages have separate headings with PDF-page numbers.
- Editorial notes, explanations and corrections are explicitly labelled blockquotes.
- Lectures 31–50 distinguish printed-slide numbers from PDF-page numbers. PDF pages from lectures 45–50 were attributed with contact sheets labelled by source filename and page on every tile; every referenced final crop was opened individually.
- Restored hypotheses, source-typo corrections and supplementary proofs are explicitly labelled as editorial, not attributed to the PDF.
